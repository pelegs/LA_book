# Structure idea for a complete linear algebra book

## Part 1: Mathematical Background
This part will focus on providing overviews of relevant background material.
It should be thorough enough such that anyone reading it will not have to search other
material to understand this book completely, but also concise enough to essentially be a review.

Chapters:

### Sets
### Functions
### Trigonometry
### ...

## Part 2: Basic Linear Algebra
This part will cover the most basic LA concepts, starting with the (real, geometric) 2- and 3-dimensional cases,
and generalizing to (real) n-dimensions after the 2/3-dims cases are covered.

Chapters:

### Vectors
### Geometric Objects
### Linear Transformations
### Matrices
### Linear Equations
### Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues
### Matrix Decompositions
### Principle Component Aalysis? (maybe in next Part)
