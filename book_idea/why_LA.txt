This is a not-at-all-comprehensive list of science fields/topics that directly use linear algebra:

* Physics: LITERALLY EVERYTHING. It's hard to think about a field in physics that doesn't use linear algebra. Basic quantum physics, for example, is formulated *entirely* in the language of LA. Special relativity uses LA extensively, general relativity is formulated using differential geometry, which in turn needs tensor algebra for its formulation - and TA is one extension of LA. Fluid dynamics, dynamic systems, statistical mechanics, etc., etc. - all use LA and maths based on LA all the time.

* Chemistry: obviously, anything that uses physics directly, e.g. quantum chemistry, physical chemistry, etc.

* Biology: TBA

* Computer science: machine learning (and especially LLMs), quantum computing, cryptography, ... - all use LA extensively.

* Engineering: electric engineering, mechanical engineering, aerospace engineering, etc. - LA is everywhere.
