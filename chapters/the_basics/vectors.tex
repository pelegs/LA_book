\section{Vectors and Vector Spaces}
\begin{summary}{}{}
	This section presents the two most fundemantal concepts in linear algebra: \newterm{vectors} and \newterm{vector spaces}.

	We will:
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item introduce vectors, their properties and basic operations between vectors.
		\item Learn how vectors can be described algebraicly using coordinate systems/basis sets.
		\item See how we can define vector spaces and vector-subspaces, and their uses.
		\item And finally, we will describe the geometric meaning of all these structures and how they relate to analytical geometry in 2- and 3-dimensions.
	\end{enumerate}
\end{summary}
