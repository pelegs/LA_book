\section*{Why Write Yet Another Book About Linear Algebra?}
The direct answer to this question is simple: I really think most resources are doing a \textit{bad job} of explaining fundamental linear algebra to new students \footnote{and honestly, this isn't restricted to linear algebra but applies to all fundamental mathematical topics - but it's a classic case}. Not because they lack material or the complete picture  - but because the common approach to teaching fundamental mathematical topics just doesn't work well for most people \footnote{the main exception is students of pure mathematics and people who can easily deal with extremely abstract concepts - a very specific group of people at the end of the day}.

Introduction courses to fundamental mathematical topics usually look like this: here'e are some basic definitions, let's derive some specific results from them - now go apply these in your field.
